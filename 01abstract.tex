\begin{abstract}
 An application of the International Swaps and Derivatives Association's Standard Initial Margin Model (SIMM) to margining in decentralised finance (DeFi). In this first iteration we focus on the most popular case, tokenised funds. We apply published risk weights to derive loan-to-value (LTV) limits, liquidatable loan-to-value (LLTV), and the borrow and supply caps. The liquidity horizon for tokenised funds is assumed to be that set by the primary dealer, or issuer, since secondary markets rely on trust assumptions, and remain sparse. We derive margins that accomodate the appropriate haircuts for warehousing the risk of liquidators on these assets. Some free parameters remain free to choose, but the aim is to reduce the impact of discretion on leverage in DeFi as far as possible, by setting transparent limits.
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}
 An application of the International Swaps and Derivatives Association's Standard Initial Margin Model (SIMM) to margining in decentralised finance (DeFi). We consider tokenised funds and linear, long-only tokenised single equities. We apply published risk weights to derive loan-to-value (LTV) limits, liquidatable loan-to-value (LLTV), and the borrow and supply caps. The liquidity horizon for tokenised funds is assumed to be that set by the primary dealer, or issuer, since secondary markets rely on trust assumptions, and remain sparse. For equities, the whole supply cap and the underlying share's trading volume determine the sale horizon. We derive margins that accommodate the appropriate haircuts for warehousing the risk of liquidators on these assets. Some free parameters remain free to choose, but the aim is to reduce the impact of discretion on leverage in DeFi as far as possible, by setting transparent limits.
\end{abstract}

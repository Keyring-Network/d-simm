% A) Symbols
\section{Symbols}
Notation for the methodology. SIMM quantities ($WS$, $K_b$,
$\rho$, $\gamma$, $\psi$, $\mathrm{CR}$) follow ISDA SIMM v2.6
\cite{isda_simm_v2_6}. None of these prescribed parameters is fitted.
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.18\linewidth} p{0.77\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
$\theta$ & Liquidation threshold (LLTV); $\theta=\kappa(1-H)$ ($=1-H$ at Prime), with $H_{\mathrm{eff}}=1-\theta\ge H$.\\
$\ell_{\max}$ & Maximum borrow LTV; $\ell_{\max} = \theta - \nu$.\\
$\nu$ & Per-listing liquidation buffer between LLTV and LTV (4--8\,pp); static rather than dynamic.\\
$\Delta t_A$ & Longest gap between usable published valuations in business days: the attested valuation publication frequency (the publication cycle of Section~\ref{sec:theory:setting}) plus the attested publication lag. Debt accrues across it every calendar day, so the buffer requirement takes the year fraction $\Delta t_A/252$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:notch-buffer}).\\
$H$ & Total collateral haircut; $H = \mathrm{IM} + \delta_{\mathrm{clear}} + \delta_{\mathrm{carry}} + \Lambda$.\\
$\mathrm{IM}$ & Delta component of ISDA SIMM v2.6 initial margin per unit of published valuation at SIMM's native horizon of ten business days (no MPOR rescaling), aggregated over risk classes IR, CreditQ, CreditNonQ, Equity, FX and summed over product classes (Eq.~\eqref{eq:psi}).\\
$\mathrm{IM}_p$ & Margin of SIMM product class $p$, RatesFX and Credit among them: the $\psi$ roll-up over the risk classes it holds. The product-class margins add, $\mathrm{IM}=\sum_p \mathrm{IM}_p$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:psi}).\\
$\delta_{\mathrm{clear}}$ & Clearing-discount component of $H$, above the liquidation threshold: the difference between the price risk of the governing clearing window and the SIMM charge at ten business days. Day one $\delta_{\mathrm{clear}}=\mathrm{IM}(\sqrt{LH/10}-1)$, a charge for $LH>10$ and a margin credit for $LH<10$, read from contractually enforceable terms, attested or documented. Estimated legs and FRTB fallback windows never earn a credit. Once $\ge N_{\mathrm{obs}}$ qualified clearing observations exist, $\mathrm{IM}+\delta_{\mathrm{clear}}=\max(\mathrm{IM}\sqrt{LH/10},\,\delta_{\mathrm{obs}})$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:m-scaling}). The discount any clearing party receives is bounded by $\mathrm{IM}+\delta_{\mathrm{clear}}+\delta_{\mathrm{carry}}$, inside $H$.\\
$\delta_{\mathrm{obs}}$ & Conservative quantile of the qualified clearing observations inside the practitioner's lookback, as a total clearing cost per unit of published valuation.\\
$N_{\mathrm{obs}}$ & Number of qualified clearing observations that must exist before $\delta_{\mathrm{obs}}$ can govern the price-risk charge (Eq.~\eqref{eq:m-scaling}). The practitioner sets it with the quantile, the lookback, and the diversity requirement, as the standard for a qualified clearing observation (free parameter, Appendix~\ref{sec:free-parameters}).\\
$\delta_{\mathrm{carry}}$ & Interest-cost component of $H$: the certain cost of holding the debt over the clearing window, $\delta_{\mathrm{carry}} = \theta\, r^{B,\max}\, LH/252$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:delta-carry}), the ceiling annual percentage yield charged simple over the window's calendar span of $LH\times365/252$ days, and charged only after liquidation begins. It never enters $\mathrm{IM}$, $\nu$, or $\lambda_{\mathrm{gate}}$, and is computed in closed form at Eq.~\eqref{eq:carry-solve}.\\
$LH$ & Governing clearing window in business days (Eq.~\eqref{eq:mpor-eff}): the sum of the four legs $w+d_n+d_d+d_s$, with the three-source hierarchy and its floors fixed in Section~\ref{sec:theory:setting}. The window composes across a fund-of-funds wrapper's and the underlying fund's notice, dealing, and settlement chains at the documented worst case, and terms are never averaged. The window is never shorter than one business day.\\
$\Lambda$ & Sum of additive haircut layers; $\Lambda = \lambda_{\mathrm{gate}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{stale}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{oracle}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{def}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{prep}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{ops}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{ww}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{lev}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{event}}$.\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{gate}}$ & Gating/suspension layer.\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{stale}}$, $\lambda^{\mathrm{base}}_{\mathrm{stale}}$, $\Delta\lambda_{\mathrm{stale}}$ & Valuation staleness/smoothing layer, $\lambda_{\mathrm{stale}} = \lambda^{\mathrm{base}}_{\mathrm{stale}} + \Delta\lambda_{\mathrm{stale}}$: the per-type baseline for the ordinary age of an on-time publication, plus the overdue-publication escalation of Eq.~\eqref{eq:stale}, which is zero while publication stays within the contractual publication cycle.\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{oracle}}$ & Oracle pipeline integrity layer; set by the oracle-configuration tier, $0$ at Robust.\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{def}}$, $\lambda^{\mathrm{floor}}_{\mathrm{def}}$ & Default add-on, $\mathrm{PD}\times\mathrm{LGD}$ over the clearing window $LH$ (private credit only), floored at the practitioner's controlled per-type value $\lambda^{\mathrm{floor}}_{\mathrm{def}}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:def-floor}); zero for the types whose default tail is booked in another layer.\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{prep}}$ & Prepayment add-on (amortising mortgage/consumer securitised collateral).\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{ops}}$ & Manager/operational layer; its value is a practitioner-set model constant for each type.\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{ww}}$ & Wrong-way layer.\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{lev}}$ & Leverage add-on (fund-unit collateral only).\\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{event}}$ & Event-risk add-on (event-linked ILS collateral only): $\max$ of the attested 99\% annual-aggregate modelled valuation loss (AEP, ultimate, net of fund hedges) and the largest attested modelled single-event valuation impact; floored at a prescribed conservative value when the event-model file is unattested or uncorroborated.\\
$\lambda_j^{\mathrm{eff}}$, $R_{\mathrm{ins},j}$ & Additive layer $\lambda_j$ after any qualifying-insurance credit, and the recognised reduction itself: $\lambda_j^{\mathrm{eff}} = \max(\phi_j\lambda_j,\;\lambda_j - R_{\mathrm{ins},j})$ with $R_{\mathrm{ins},j} = \beta_j\,\lambda_j\,\min(1,\mathrm{NetLimit}/E_{\mathrm{ref}})$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:insurance-credit}). Eligible only against the manager/operational layer $\lambda_{\mathrm{ops}}$ and the oracle-integrity layer $\lambda_{\mathrm{oracle}}$, one policy to one layer (Section~\ref{sec:policy-insurance}).\\
$\beta_j$, $\phi_j$ & Prescribed recognition factor for the policy's evidenced quality, $\beta_j\in[0,1]$, and the prescribed floor fraction below which the credit never takes the layer. Both are practitioner elections, and the credit ships inert at $\beta_j=0$ until they are set (free parameter, Appendix~\ref{sec:free-parameters}).\\
$\mathrm{NetLimit}$, $E_{\mathrm{ref}}$ & The policy's per-occurrence limit \emph{net} of deductibles, retentions, shared-limit allocation, and excluded perils, and the reference exposure it is measured against, $E_{\mathrm{ref}}^{(a)}=\max(\text{live exposure},\,S_{\max}^{(a)})$.\\
$r$, $r_{\mathrm{floor}}$ & Credit grade entering the default add-on, and the prescribed floor grade applied where no admissible rating exists (Eq.~\eqref{eq:rating-hierarchy}). A rating from a nationally recognized statistical rating organization governs where one exists, a corroborated manager internal rating $r_{\mathrm{int}}$ resolves one grade weaker and no better than BB$+$, and the practitioner sets $r_{\mathrm{floor}}$ (free parameter, Appendix~\ref{sec:free-parameters}).\\
$\mathrm{PD}_1(r)$ & Published one-year cohort default rate at grade $r$, read from rating-agency default tables and never fitted.\\
$\mathrm{PD}(r,LH)$ & Probability of default over the clearing window, $\mathrm{PD}(r,LH)=1-(1-\mathrm{PD}_1(r))^{LH/252}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:pd-horizon}): the survival form of a constant default intensity on a 252-business-day year, evaluated only at $LH<252$.\\
$\mathrm{LGD}$, $\mathrm{LGD}_{\mathrm{floor}}$ & Loss given default, the fraction of exposure not recovered, read from a published senior-secured recovery table keyed to attested seniority. The prescribed FRTB default-risk-charge values are 75\% for senior debt and 100\% for non-senior debt or an equity instrument. Where seniority is unattested the claim takes the non-senior value, $\mathrm{LGD}_{\mathrm{floor}}=100\%$ (free parameter, Appendix~\ref{sec:free-parameters}).\\
$R_{\mathrm{cap}}$ & Attested issuer redemption capacity in USD per dealing window, window-normalised where the attestation basis differs, refreshed at the attestation frequency.\\
$g$ & The offering documents' stated per-cycle gate fraction, used in the pre-signature capacity proxy $g\cdot\mathrm{AUM}$.\\
$B_{\max}$ & Borrow cap, $B_{\max} \le R_{\mathrm{cap}}$, visible in the on-chain market configuration. Where the deployment can attribute debt to entities, the largest observed single-entity debt is monitored as $B_{(1)} \le R_{\mathrm{cap}}$, a diagnostic rather than an assumption.\\
$S_{\max}$ & Supply cap; $S_{\max} \le M\, R_{\mathrm{cap}}$.\\
$M$ & Supply-cap multiple of redemption capacity; bounds the concentration risk factor at $\sqrt{M}$ (free parameter; $8$ illustrative).\\
$T$ & SIMM concentration threshold, re-keyed to redemption capacity: $T = R_{\mathrm{cap}}$.\\
$\mathrm{CR}$ & Concentration risk factor, $\mathrm{CR} = \max\!\big(1, \sqrt{\mathrm{position}/T}\big)$.\\
$WS_k$ & Weighted sensitivity of risk factor $k$: $WS_k = RW_k\, s_k\, \mathrm{CR}_b$ (published risk weight $\times$ attested sensitivity $\times$ concentration risk factor).\\
$K_b$ & Within-bucket aggregate margin, $K_b = \sqrt{\bigl(\sum_k WS_k^2 + \sum_{k \ne l} \rho_{kl} f_{kl} WS_k WS_l\bigr)_{\!+}}$.\\
$(x)_+$ & Positive part, $\max(x,0)$, applied to the sum under the root in $K_b$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:kb}).\\
$\rho_{kl}$ & Prescribed within-bucket risk-factor correlation.\\
$f_{kl}$ & SIMM concentration factor, $f_{kl} = \min(\mathrm{CR}_k,\mathrm{CR}_l)/\max(\mathrm{CR}_k,\mathrm{CR}_l)$.\\
$\gamma_{bc}$ & Prescribed across-bucket correlation, applied to bucket sums $S_b$ bounded to $[-K_b, K_b]$.\\
$\psi$ & Prescribed across-risk-class correlation matrix, applied to the risk classes a SIMM product class holds before the product-class margins add to $\mathrm{IM}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:psi}).\\
$\kappa$ & Due-diligence tier multiplier scaling $\theta$, $\kappa\in(0,1]$ with the top tier at $1$ (free parameter; $\{1.00,0.90,0.75\}$ illustrative).\\
$r^{B,\max}$ & Ceiling of the deployed market's borrow-rate model: its annual percentage yield at full utilisation, on a 365-day calendar basis, at which the position's debt is taken to accrue every calendar day. A kink rate qualifies only where no higher rate is attainable. The deployed market configuration supplies this input between recomputations, and the buffer requirement uses it rather than the live borrow rate $r^B$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:notch-buffer}).\\
$c$ & Interest cost per unit of debt over the clearing window, $c = r^{B,\max}\,LH/252$. It enters the closed-form solve $H = (\mathrm{IM}+\delta_{\mathrm{clear}}+\Lambda+\kappa c)/(1+\kappa c)$ with $\delta_{\mathrm{carry}} = \theta c$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:carry-solve}).\\
$J$, $q_p(J)$ & Jump at the next published valuation and the prescribed per-type stressed-jump floor used in the buffer requirement: the lower $p$-quantile of that jump, set as policy and never fitted to the token's own valuation history (Eq.~\eqref{eq:notch-buffer}).\\
$\hat\sigma$ & Staleness volatility implied by the type SIMM calibration (Eq.~\eqref{eq:stale}).\\
$\rho_{\mathrm{sub}}$ & Published SIMM sub-curve correlation between distinct sub-curves of one currency ($=0.993$); enters only where distinct sub-curves of one currency are held, for example a USD Prime sub-curve on securitised collateral.\\
MPOR & Margin period of risk: the horizon over which margin must cover the collateral's price move.\\
$w$, $d_n$, $d_d$, $d_s$ & The four legs of $LH$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:mpor-eff}), in business days: the worst-case wait to the next submission deadline, and the attested notice period, dealing window, and settlement lag.\\
$H_0$ & The haircut before the interest cost, $H_0 = \mathrm{IM}+\delta_{\mathrm{clear}}+\Lambda$, so that $\delta_{\mathrm{carry}} = H - H_0$ exactly (Eq.~\eqref{eq:carry-solve}).\\
$Q_{\mathrm{cap,red}}$, $Q_{\mathrm{issuer}}$, $Q_{\mathrm{queue}}$ & Redemption capacity over one dealing window and its two terms: the issuer capacity term equals $R_{\mathrm{cap}}$, and the committed-settlement term is the within-window processing level the subscription documents commit to, zero where they commit to no measurable level (Eqs.~\eqref{eq:qcapred} and~\eqref{eq:queue}).\\
$q_L^{\mathrm{committed}}$ & The committed settlement level for one window, read from the subscription documents case by case per listing and recorded as an attested field, or the higher attested commitment where one exists. The feasibility check passes only where it covers the borrow cap.\\
$q_L$, $q_O^{*}$, $A$ & The redemption request submitted for the position in liquidation, the stressed concurrent third-party demand on the same window, and the fund AUM. The window fills $\min\{q_L+q_O^{*},\,g\,A\}$, and $q_O^{*}$ takes the conservative floor $q_O^{*}=g\,A$, a fully subscribed window, where the transfer agent does not report queue depth and gate utilisation (Eq.~\eqref{eq:queue}).\\
$B_{(1)}$, $B_{\mathrm{tot}}^{(a)}$ & Largest observed single-entity debt against a collateral, and total outstanding debt against it, $B_{\mathrm{tot}}^{(a)}=\sum_e B_e^{(a)}$.\\
$\mathrm{CovR}$, $u$ & Coverage ratio $\mathrm{CovR} = R_{\mathrm{cap}}/\sum_e B_e$ and its reciprocal, the utilisation $u = 1/\mathrm{CovR}$: aggregate borrows against one window of redemption capacity.\\
$S_{\mathrm{global}}^{\text{(issuer)}}$ & Global supply cap per issuer, bounding the sum of per-deployment supply caps, since $R_{\mathrm{cap}}$ is an issuer-level resource that does not multiply with the number of listings (Section~\ref{sec:policy-issuer}).\\
NMRF & Non-modellable risk factor, the FRTB treatment of a risk factor without usable data \cite{bcbs_frtb_d457}. D-SIMM follows it in the missing-data rule: an unavailable input takes a prescribed conservative floor, never zero (Section~\ref{sec:agg-missing}).\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
